# Topical Authority — trowel.id

## Role and boundary

`trowel.id` is the Indonesian trade, tool, and process hub for work performed with hand trowels and concrete power trowels. It should help applicators, operators, supervisors, estimators, designers, buyers, and owners choose the right tool or machine, coordinate substrate and material readiness, supervise a safe finishing sequence, verify quality, diagnose defects, and procure competent work.

The domain owns the **application interface**: what the trowel or finishing machine does, when the surface is ready, how adjoining work affects the operation, which checkpoints prove the result, and when work must stop for a competent person. It may explain concrete, screed, render, mortar, tile adhesive, self-levelling products, dry-shake hardener, and resin flooring only as they affect trowel selection or application. It does not replace full structural concrete design, generic flooring-system design, plaster formulation, chemical-product formulation, or epoxy chemistry.

Neutral education and transactional pages must remain distinct. `/finishing-trowel/` owns the power-trowel service offer, `/floor-hardener/` owns the dry-shake floor-hardener service offer, `/kontak-kami/` owns direct contact, and the homepage is the domain/service gateway. Planned knowledge articles may link to those routes when a reader has reached a legitimate procurement decision, but they do not make unsupported price, performance, nationwide-coverage, or “best contractor” claims.

Cannibalization is evaluated only among URLs on **trowel.id**. Syamsul-owned domains are independent editorial properties and may cover the same query from the same or another viewpoint. Cross-domain overlap is an opportunity for complementary treatment, not a reason to suppress useful coverage here.

## Existing coverage and risks

- **Same-domain collision:** `/`, `/finishing-trowel/`, and `/floor-hardener/` all target trowel plus floor-hardener service intent. The two service pages repeat definitions, benefits, types, quantities, brands, application, pricing, and calls to action.
- **Mixed intent:** neutral definitions and potentially hazardous execution guidance sit inside sales pages. Education, operator training, and commercial scope have no clear owners.
- **Unsupported and volatile claims:** legacy prices, coverage across all Indonesia, material-consumption figures, slip/dust/oil resistance, load claims, testimonials, and “professional/expert” language require current product, project, or business evidence.
- **Safety gaps:** existing copy gives fresh-concrete timing cues and application statements without strong gates for operator competence, guarding, emergency stops, ventilation, carbon monoxide, fuel, electrical sources, wet-cement burns, chemical SDS, silica during rework, ergonomics, exclusion zones, lifting, or lockout.

- **Template and trust risk:** `/tentang-kami/` contains unrelated placeholder copy and invented-looking metrics; this damages credibility until replaced or temporarily noindexed.
- **Sitemap integrity:** the older six-entry sitemap publishes an unrelated malformed URL while the complete sitemap lists only five valid routes.
- **Thin architecture:** there is no article route pattern, knowledge hub, topic taxonomy, breadcrumb model, or internal-link path from diagnosis to prevention, repair, and procurement.

## Related-domain opportunities

- `concrete.co.id` may own mix design, reinforcement, slab/joint design, curing science, concrete testing, and structural performance. `trowel.id` should cover only the readiness and finishing interface, then link readers to qualified concrete guidance where useful.
- `lantai.id` may compare complete flooring systems, substrate assemblies, performance, lifecycle, and room/use choices. `trowel.id` should explain the trowel or smoothing-tool task inside those systems.
- `epoxy.co.id` and `epoxylantai.co.id` may cover resin chemistry, full epoxy-floor system design, substrate qualification, coatings, and repair. `trowel.id` may independently explain notched/smoothing trowel selection, application interface, coverage, operator exposure, and handoff.
- `kontraktorepoxy.co.id` may own epoxy-contractor assessment and commercial execution. `trowel.id` may still compare trowel-service scope and equipment/operator evidence on its own domain.
- Cross-domain overlap is acceptable. Do not create a network of manipulative exact-match links or copy text. Link only when it completes a reader task, and label whether the destination is neutral education or a commercial route.
- No local/city series is planned. A geographic article is justified only by real climate, logistics, rules, supply, service capability, or documented project evidence that changes the substance.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Treat legacy copy as a lead, not a source. Verify every price, dosage, consumption, load, abrasion, dust, slip, oil, productivity, nationwide coverage, brand, warranty, testimonial, and project claim before publication.
- For Indonesian K3 content, start with current official sources. The official JDIH record currently marks [Permenaker No. 5 Tahun 2018 tentang K3 Lingkungan Kerja](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/peraturan/detail/1546/peraturan-menteri-nomor-5-tahun-2018) as in force; recheck status and applicability at publication time and have a competent K3 reviewer interpret duties.
- Machine-specific operation and maintenance must cite the exact current manufacturer manual for the model. Manuals such as Multiquip’s [walk-behind operation manual](https://www.multiquip.com/multiquip/pdfs/B46B90_rev_1_ops_manual.pdf) and [ride-on operation manual](https://www.multiquip.com/multiquip/pdfs/Trowels_ride_on_mechanical_HTN27K_28K_Rev_2_ops_manual_DataId_19192_Version_4.pdf) demonstrate why training, controls, safety-stop checks, lifting, fuel, service, and model differences cannot be replaced with generic SEO instructions.
- Silica content must use exposure assessment and hierarchy of controls. NIOSH identifies concrete and mortar as silica-containing materials and prioritizes working engineering controls, wet methods, and local exhaust where appropriate; see [NIOSH silica safe work practices](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/silica/work/index.html). Do not turn a foreign exposure limit into an Indonesian legal claim.
- Fresh cement/concrete content must warn about delayed caustic burns and dermatitis and use PPE/work-practice review; see [OSHA concrete hazard guidance](https://www.osha.gov/concrete-products/controlling-hazards). It is not medical advice.
- Fuel-engine content must treat carbon monoxide as a stop-critical hazard in enclosed or partly enclosed areas. OSHA specifically includes trowels among fuel-powered tools implicated in CO exposure; see its [portable gas-powered equipment guidance](https://www.osha.gov/vtools/construction/carbon-monoxide-fnl-eng-web-transcript). “Open a door and add fans” is not a universal safe-work answer.
- Chemical content requires the current product TDS and SDS, substrate and ambient limits, compatible PPE, ventilation, spill/waste plan, and applicator review. Never infer resin, hardener, solvent, or cement chemistry from a product category name.
- Concrete-floor technique and acceptance require the project specification and qualified reviewer. ACI’s [concrete-finisher competency task analysis](https://www.concrete.org/Portals/0/Files/PDF/SCICF-JTA.pdf) may guide topic coverage, but paid standards, local specifications, manufacturer requirements, and project engineer decisions must not be paraphrased as universal law.
- Every execution article states prerequisites, competent-person role, hold points, stop conditions, verification, and escalation. It never gives a universal clock, pitch, speed, pass count, water addition, material broadcast rate, electrical connection, refuelling shortcut, guard bypass, or improvised repair.
- Field claims require original dated evidence: site conditions, material batch/product, equipment model, readings, approved sample, before/during/after photos, deviations, corrective action, and client permission. Do not fabricate a case study.
- All standards, regulations, TDS/SDS, manuals, product availability, prices, and contacts get a retrieval or verification date and owner for future review.
