# Article Catalog — trowel.id

This catalog plans the complete editorial space for trowel-applied construction work on `trowel.id`. It expands 15 parent topics into 90 distinct briefs. It is a roadmap, not permission to mass-publish thin pages.

Domain boundary: the catalog owns hand-trowel and power-trowel selection, the application interface with concrete/screed/render/flooring materials, controlled execution and supervision, K3, QA, defects, equipment care, and procurement. It does not replace structural concrete design, complete flooring-system design, plaster or resin formulation, project engineering, manufacturer instructions, SDS, operator training, or site-specific K3 planning. `/finishing-trowel/` and `/floor-hardener/` remain the transactional service owners.

Catalog statistics: **15 topics**, **90 article briefs**, **6 briefs per topic**, **12 P0**, **44 P1**, **33 P2**, and **1 P3**. Wave 1 contains 12 connected assets; later waves are released only after review capacity and evidence exist.

Priority legend: **P0** first bounded cluster and safety/intent foundation; **P1** high-value lifecycle or risk coverage; **P2** important depth after the foundation; **P3** specialist, evidence-heavy, or lower-frequency coverage.
